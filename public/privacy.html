Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Global Exam Practice ("we", "our", or "the Website") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, how that information is used, and how it is stored.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process limited information to provide and improve our exam practice services.

Anonymous Browser Identifier

When you use the Website, a randomly generated browser identifier may be stored in your browser's local storage.

This identifier is used to associate your exam attempts and results with the same browser. It is not designed to identify you personally.

Exam and Answer Information

When you participate in a practice or mock exam, we may store information such as:

  • Exam and question identifiers
  • Exam mode and selected language
  • Selected answers and answer correctness
  • Number of questions and correct answers
  • Exam scores and pass status
  • Exam start and completion timestamps

This information allows us to calculate exam results and provide access to your exam history.

Technical Information

When you access the Website, technical information may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers to deliver and secure the service. This may include information associated with website requests, browsers, and devices.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide online exam practice and mock tests.
  • Calculate and display exam results.
  • Maintain exam history associated with your browser.
  • Support website functionality and security.
  • Identify and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Improve the Website and user experience.

We do not require users to create an account or provide their real names to use the basic exam practice features.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

The Website uses browser local storage to remember your language preference and generate a random browser identifier.

These identifiers help the Website remember your preferences and associate exam attempts with your browser.

You can clear local storage through your browser settings. However, doing so may remove your saved language preference and prevent the Website from recognizing previously recorded exam history associated with your previous browser identifier.

4. Third-Party Services

The Website is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process technical information necessary to deliver, maintain, and protect the Website.

We do not currently use Google Analytics or Google AdSense.

If additional third-party analytics or advertising services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

5. Data Retention and Security

Exam attempt information is stored in our database to support exam results and history features.

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the Website. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Clearing your browser's local storage does not automatically delete exam records already stored on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

The Website provides educational practice resources and may be used by learners of different ages.

We do not intentionally request personal information such as a user's real name, home address, or telephone number through the basic exam practice features.

Parents or guardians who have questions about information processed through the Website should contact us through our available contact channel.

7. Your Choices

You may clear the Website's local storage through your browser settings.

Please note that clearing local storage may affect language preferences and access to exam history associated with your previous browser identifier.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Website's features and services evolve.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact us through our designated contact channel.
