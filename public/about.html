About Global Exam Practice

Global Exam Practice is a free online learning resource that helps learners explore practice questions and prepare for exams around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make exam preparation more accessible by bringing practice resources together in one convenient place. We aim to help learners review important topics, check their understanding, and build confidence at their own pace.

Explore and Practice

Browse available exams by country and region, select a practice test, and work through questions at your own pace. Our resources cover different exam categories, with new practice materials added over time.

Educational Purpose

All practice materials are provided for educational and informational purposes. Practice questions may not be identical to questions on official examinations. We encourage learners to consult the relevant official materials when preparing for an exam.

Independent Resource

Global Exam Practice is an independent educational resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any government agency, licensing authority, or examination provider.
